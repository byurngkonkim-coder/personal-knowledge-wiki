Question #21

Is she going to educate the staff this week, or next?

We'd better go and ask Jane. (가서 Jane에게 물어보는 것이 낫겠네요.)

She didn't mention it yet. (아직 그것에 관해서 언급하지 않았어요.)

She has not decided yet. (그녀가 아직 결정하지 않았어요.)

It depends on her schedule. (그녀의 스케줄에 따라 달라요.)

Question #22

Have you ever gone skiing this year?

No, not yet. (아뇨, 아직이요.)

I have not had a chance yet. (아직 기회가 없었어요.)

No, but I went several times last year. (아뇨, 하지만 작년에 여러 번 갔어요.)

Yes, twice already. (네, 이미 두 번이나요.)

Question #23

Question #24

Question #30

Question #32

Question #33

Question #34

Question #35
