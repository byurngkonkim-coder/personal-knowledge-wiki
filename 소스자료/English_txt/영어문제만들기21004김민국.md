★ 밑줄 친 단어와 어울리는 음식을 고르시오.

Selecting the right wines to go with a meal is an individual choice. Every person's sense of taste is different. So basically, each person should decide for him or herself what combination of wines and food tastes best. However, as an aid to starting wine-drinkers, let me offer some general guidelines: Typically, you want to drink light-to dark, just as when you plan a meal you start with delicate tastes and work towards heavier tastes. For instance, clear champagne tends to go very well with appetizers or opening courses in a meal.

White wine

is usually a match for salads and lighter dishes, while red wine works perfectly with steaks and heavier meat sauces. A port wine is often the perfect end to a meal, sipped with a piece of chocolate cake.

* port wine : 포트와인 (맛이 단 포르투갈 산의 적포도주)
