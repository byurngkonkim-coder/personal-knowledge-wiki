> ## 📚 무손실 핵심 표현 & 패턴 마스터 (Core Expressions & Patterns)
>
> ### 🔹 [문제 1] `Just in front of our office ______ a nice restaurant` : 도치 구문의 주술 일치
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**:
> - 일반적인 어순: "A nice restaurant was just in front of our office"
> - 강조와 문체적 효과를 위해 동사를 주어 앞에 배치하는 **도치(Inversion)** 구문
> - 빈 공간에는 **단수 동사 `was`**가 와야 함 (주어: "a nice restaurant" = 단수)
> - "There is/are" 구문과 동일한 원리 적용
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**:
> - "Just in front of" → "justin-fron-tof" (자연스러운 연음)
> - "was" → /wɑz/ (강세: 약한 위치)
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
> - 🇺🇸 `Just in front of the building stands a statue of the founder.`
> - 🇰🇷 건물 바로 앞에 창립자의 동상이 서 있다.
> - 🇺🇸 `Behind the door were several boxes of old documents.`
> - 🇰🇷 문 뒤에는 오래된 문서 상자들이 여러 개 있었다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
> **Teacher**: "Why is 'was' correct instead of 'were'?"
> ↳ *선생님: "왜 'were' 대신 'was'가 맞나?"*
> **Student**: "Because the subject 'a nice restaurant' is singular, so the verb must be singular."
> ↳ *학생: "주어인 'a nice restaurant'이 단수이므로 동사도 단수여야 하기 때문입니다."*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**:
> - `Just in front of + [장소] + [단수동사] + [단수주어]` → 도치 구문의 기본 틀
> - `In the corner stood a beautiful piano.` (모서리에 아름다운 피아노가 서 있었다)
> - `On the wall hung several paintings.` (벽에 여러 그림이 걸려 있었다)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**:
> - ❌ "Just in front of our office are a nice restaurant" (틀림 - 주어가 단수이므로 단수 동사 필요)
> - ✅ "Just in front of our office was a nice restaurant" (맞음)
> - **실수**: 한국어에서는 "있다"가 주어 뒤에 오므로, 영어도 비슷할 것으로 착각하기 쉬움

> ### 🔹 [문제 2] `______ it not been for Mr. Gates' innovative leadership` : 가정법 과거완료 도치
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**:
> - 표준 형태: "If it had not been for..." (만약 ~이 없었다면)
> - 도치 형태: "Had it not been for..." (격식적, 문어체)
> - **가정법 과거완료**는 과거에 일어나지 않은 일에 대한 가정을 나타냄
> - "~이 없었다면, (현재) ~일 것이다" = 과거가 현재 상황에 영향을 미친 경우
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**:
> - "Had it" → "had-it" (자연스러운 연음, h 약하게 발음)
> - "not been" → "nah-bin" (축약형 같은 발음)
> - 전체 강세: **Had** ↑ (강세)
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
> - 🇺🇸 `Had it not been for your help, I would never have succeeded.`
> - 🇰🇷 너의 도움이 없었다면, 나는 절대 성공하지 못했을 것이다.
> - 🇺🇸 `Had the weather been better, we would have gone to the beach.`
> - 🇰🇷 날씨가 더 좋았다면, 우리는 해변에 갔을 것이다.
> - 🇺🇸 `Without the CEO's strategic vision, MicroLingua Corp. would not be successful today.` (비도치 형태)
> - 🇰🇷 CEO의 전략적 비전이 없었다면, MicroLingua Corp.은 오늘날 성공하지 못했을 것이다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
> **Manager**: "How did you manage to turn the company around?"
> ↳ *매니저: "어떻게 회사를 부활시킬 수 있었나?"*
> **Director**: "Had it not been for the team's dedication, I couldn't have done it."
> ↳ *이사: "팀의 헌신이 없었다면, 저는 그것을 할 수 없었을 것입니다."*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**:
> - `Had it not been for + [요인], S + would + have + [과거분사]`
> - `Had she not warned me, I would have made a terrible mistake.` (그녀가 경고하지 않았다면, 나는 끔찍한 실수를 했을 것이다)
> - `Had they invested earlier, they would have earned more profit.` (그들이 더 일찍 투자했다면, 더 많은 이익을 얻었을 것이다)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**:
> - ❌ "Was it not been for..." (틀림 - 가정법 과거완료는 'had' 사용)
> - ✅ "Had it not been for..." (맞음)
> - **혼동하기 쉬운 점**: "If it had not been" vs. "Had it not been" - 의미는 같지만 도치는 더 격식적

> ### 🔹 [문제 3] `it was not so much business manager _______ the executive director` : 부정 구조 with "not so much...as"
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**:
> - "not so much A as B" = "A라기보다는 B가 (더 진짜다)" = "주로 B의 책임"
> - 선택지: (A) but (B) than (C) as (D) from
> - 정답: (C) **as** → "not so much A **as** B" 구조
> - 함축: 비즈니스 매니저도 반대했지만, **실제로는 이사진이 주요 반대자**였음
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**:
> - "not so much" → "nah-so-much" (자연스러운 강세)
> - "business manager" → "BIZ-nis MANager" (BIZ와 MAN에 강세)
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
> - 🇺🇸 `It was not so much the cost as the timing that concerned us.`
> - 🇰🇷 비용이라기보다는 타이밍이 우리를 우려케 했다.
> - 🇺🇸 `She doesn't blame the employees so much as the poor management system.`
> - 🇰🇷 그녀는 직원들보다는 형편없는 관리 시스템을 탓한다.
> - 🇺🇸 `The problem was not so much what was said as how it was said.`
> - 🇰🇷 문제는 무엇이 말해졌는가가 아니라, 어떻게 말해졌는가였다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
> **Analyst**: "Who was primarily responsible for the decision?"
> ↳ *분석가: "누가 주로 그 결정에 책임이 있었나?"*
> **Colleague**: "It was not so much the business manager as the executive director who opposed it."
> ↳ *동료: "그것을 반대한 것은 비즈니스 매니저라기보다는 이사진이었습니다."*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**:
> - `not so much + [A] + as + [B]` = "A 보다는 B가 더 (진짜)"
> - `It's not so much laziness as lack of motivation.` (게으름이라기보다는 동기 부족)
> - `The issue is not so much with the product as with the marketing strategy.` (문제는 제품이 아니라 마케팅 전략)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**:
> - ❌ "not so much...but" (틀림 - "not so much...as"가 올바른 표현)
> - ❌ "not so much...than" (틀림 - "than"은 단순 비교급, "as"는 대조 관계 표현)
> - ✅ "not so much...as" (맞음)
>

> ### 🔹 [문제 4] `_____ the time frame and budget are concerned, the plan sounds perfect` : 제한 표현 "As far as"
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**:
> - "As far as ~" = "~에 한한 범위에서", "~의 한에 있어서는"
> - 선택지: (A) As long as (B) As much as (C) As far as (D) As well as
> - 정답: (C) **As far as** → "~에 관한 한", "~의 측면에서는"
> - **As far as X is concerned** = "X에 관해서는", "X의 입장에서는"
> - "As long as" ≠ "As far as": As long as = "동안에", "조건이라면" (시간/조건)
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**:
> - "As far as" → "az-far-az" (3개 강세 균등)
> - "are concerned" → "are con-CERNED" (CERNED에 강세)
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
> - 🇺🇸 `As far as I'm concerned, the proposal is excellent.`
> - 🇰🇷 제 입장에서는, 그 제안은 훌륭합니다.
> - 🇺🇸 `As far as the deadline is concerned, we need to accelerate our efforts.`
> - 🇰🇷 기한이라는 측면에서는, 우리는 우리의 노력을 가속화할 필요가 있습니다.
> - 🇺🇸 `As far as budget constraints are concerned, this project is viable.`
> - 🇰🇷 예산 제약이라는 관점에서는, 이 프로젝트는 실행 가능합니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
> **CFO**: "Can we approve this project?"
> ↳ *재무이사: "이 프로젝트를 승인할 수 있을까?"*
> **Project Manager**: "As far as the budget is concerned, yes. As far as the timeline is concerned, it might be tight."
> ↳ *프로젝트 매니저: "예산이라는 측면에서는, 네. 일정이라는 측면에서는, 타이트할 수 있습니다."*

1. Just in front of our office ______ a nice restaurant where they served a very good    reindeer steak. 

(A) was(B) be(C) to be(D) had been

2. ______ it not been for Mr. Gates’ innovative leadership, MicroLingua Corp. would not be     what it is today.

(A) Was(B) Were(C) Has(D) Had

3. I hear it was not so much business manager _______ the executive director that opposed     making alterations in the terms of the contract.

(A) but(B) than(C) as(D) from

4. _____ the time frame and budget are concerned, the plan for the project sounds perfect. 

(A) As long as(B) As much as(C) As far as(D) As well as

5. We should investigate the case _____ until we have sufficient information to make a     judgement.

(A) further(B) so far(C) far enough(D) by far

6. Classmates.com is one of _______ net properties in the U.S., ranking 16th in the league     of most-visited websites. 

(A) the hottest(B) hotter(C) as hot as(D) hot as any

7. We will need ______ 150 people to carry out the plan in that short time.

(A) quite a few(B) no more than(C) as many as(D) quite a bit

8. We need to reexamine our business strategy; our sales this quarter were ______     two-third those of the last quarter.

(A) not less than(B) no less than(C) more than(D) no more than

9. The Netherlands was bracing _____ for the sudden cold wave, and major airports were     closed.

(A) oneself(B) himself(C) itself(D) themselves

10. Several electronics companies have pulled out of the alliance and begun to go _____      own way.

(A) its(B) one’s(C) his(D) their

11. One of those scientists ______ that what was agreed on yesterday to curb hazardous      waste is not enough to make a difference. 

(A) say(B) says(C) have said(D) are saying

12. From now on, all managers are required to exercise ______ care in this matter.

(A) many (B) a lots of(C) lot of(D) much

13.

Should you (A)are to change your plan, we will (B)be pleased (C)to (D)accommodate your request and rearrange your itinerary.

14.
