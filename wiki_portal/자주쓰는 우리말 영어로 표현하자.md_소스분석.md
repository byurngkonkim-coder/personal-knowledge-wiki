##  [English | 한국어 번역] Study Material 

**A. Understanding: The English Vocabulary for Learning and Use**


###  1. Core Vocabulary (Essential & Practical Words)   
   - **For Daily Communication:** Words to build everyday conversations about your life experiences, time management, daily tasks, work/school, etc.

      * **Greetings:** Hello, How are you? Good morning/afternoon/evening, Thanks, You're welcome, Excuse me.
      * **Time & Agenda:**  Minutes, hours, today, tomorrow, next week, later, earlier. 
      * **Food & Drink:** What is that food? (Drink), coffee, tea. 
      * **Getting Around:** Travel, transportation, directions, roads, public transport, subway/metro
   *  **Useful phrases for Specific Settings: **
        - Work/Education: Meeting, assignment, deadline, presentation, test, professor, student, colleague
        - Social Gatherings: party, concert, friends' gatherings, family event, birthday parties 
       

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Core Vocabulary    

 * **"How to Learn "**   
  * **Active Practice:** Mimically copy phrases you like, read aloud, record yourself, use flashcards. 
  * **Spaced Repetition:** Review/repetition with set intervals for long-term memory retention. Don't cram!
  * **Contextual Integration:**  Put phrases into real-life scenarios for understanding and confidence (ordering at a cafe, asking directions...).

* **"How to Study More Effectively"**
    * **Focused Learning:** Identify key areas you're confused about, then review those words.  Don't just read general books - make them relatable for your life!
    * **Visual Learning:** Use dictionaries/word lists organized into categories (daily tasks, work, school), flashcards with images, or create a word-wall for frequently studied phrases.  


### 3. Advanced Vocabulary (For Deeper Understanding)

*   ** Idioms and Expressions:**  "Go the extra mile", "Break a leg!", "Put your best foot forward," "Hit the road," "Pull yourself together.” (Understanding them deeper can bring you into Korean culture/society.) 

    * **Tips for learning idioms & expressions**
       - Write down their English meanings in your dictionary. Then add examples using the target language's sentence structure to make sense of all those meanings!
       - Use an English dictionary's online tool that offers idiom explanations, as well as audio recordings and example usage.

###  4. Practice & Application 


* **Roleplay:** Act out or simulate different day-to-day scenes (Ordering food, asking for directions). The best way to understand the context of a word is to practice using it in real conversations!
* **Record yourself** and analyze what sounds clear and unclear. This helps you find your speaking points / weaknesses for further practice. 
* **Keep a journal/reflection section:** Note down any difficult words learned and how you used them. How can you apply these words/expressions in further practice?
