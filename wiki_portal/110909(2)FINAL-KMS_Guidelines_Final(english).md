> # 🗣️ 110909(2)FINAL-KMS_Guidelines_Final(english)
>
> ## 📋 요약 (Summary)
> - **주요 학습 테마**: 관세 행정 / 국제 무역 물류 / 적하목록 사전 제출 제도(AMS) / 수출입 통관 법령 / 공식 무역 서식 및 화물 식별 표기 규정
> - **핵심 수록 표현 수**: 22개 공식 법률·행정 실무 표현 및 문법 패턴 전수 수록
> - **학습 포인트**:
>   - WCO(세계관세기구) 및 관세청(KCS)의 공식 통관·물류 전문 영문 법률 및 규정 명령 표현 완전 체득
>   - 품명 기재(Goods Description) 시 허용되는 정밀 상업 표현과 반려되는 추상적 일반 명칭의 명확한 대조 습득
>   - 시한(Timeline), 의무 주체(Obliged Entity), 준수 요건(Compliance), 정정/취소(Amendment/Cancellation), 행정 제재(Sanctions) 등 공식 비즈니스 영어 패턴 마스터

> ## 📚 상세 분해 (Detailed Breakdown) — 무손실 핵심 표현 & 패턴 마스터
>
> ### 1. `Manifest` : 적하목록 (운송 수단 적재 화물 명세서)
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 선박이나 항공기에 탑재된 화물의 종합 명세서입니다. 단순한 화물 목록표(List)가 아니라 관세청이 위험 관리(Risk Management), 보세 화물 통제, 출입항 심사를 집행하는 법적 기준 문서입니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ˈmænɪfest] 첫 음절에 강세가 위치하며, 끝의 't'는 파열음으로 깔끔하게 끊어줍니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `Considered as a key document for customs administration, manifest refers to a full list of cargo carried in a sea or air transport-unit which gives particulars such as the description of import/export cargos and supply chains...`
>     - 🇰🇷 관세 행정의 핵심 서류로 간주되는 적하목록은 해상 또는 항공 운송 수단에 적재된 화물의 전체 목록을 의미하며, 수출입 화물 명세 및 공급망과 같은 세부 사항을 제공합니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Have you finalized the manifest for Flight KA801?
>   > ↳ *KA801편의 적하목록 작성을 최종 완료하셨습니까?*
>   > **B**: Yes, all cargo details and supply chain particulars are fully compiled in the manifest.
>   > ↳ *네, 모든 화물 세부 내역과 공급망 정보가 적하목록에 완전히 취합되었습니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `[Entity] must compile and submit [document]` + `manifest` = Carriers must compile and submit the manifest. (운송인은 적하목록을 작성하여 제출해야 한다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 일반 상거래 서류인 `Packing List`(포장명세서)와 혼동해서는 안 됩니다. 관세청 통관 및 운송 감독에 제출하는 공식 법정 화물 목록은 반드시 `manifest`입니다.
>
> ### 2. `Advance Manifest System (AMS)` : 적하목록 사전 제출 제도
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 화물이 선적되거나 도착하기 일정 시간 전에 적하목록 전자 데이터를 세관에 미리 전송하도록 의무화하여, 테러 방지 및 통관 속도를 높이는 글로벌 표준 통관 통제 제도입니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ədˈvæns ˈmænɪfest ˈsɪstəm] 'Advance'의 첫 모음은 약한 슈와 발음 [ə]이며, 'v'에서 윗니로 아랫입술을 가볍게 긁어줍니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `In response to the 9/11 attacks in 2001, the Advance Manifest System has been put forward as an answer to enhance logistics security, and since then it has become a global norm.`
>     - 🇰🇷 2001년 9/11 테러에 대응하여 물류 안보를 강화하기 위한 해법으로 적하목록 사전 제출 제도가 제시되었으며, 그 이후 글로벌 규범으로 자리 잡았습니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Why was the Advance Manifest System introduced in Korea?
>   > ↳ *한국에서 적하목록 사전 제출 제도가 도입된 이유는 무엇인가요?*
>   > **B**: It was adopted to comply with international standards and strengthen supply chain security.
>   > ↳ *국제 기준을 준수하고 공급망 안보를 강화하기 위해 도입되었습니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `introduce [system] to [objective]` + `the Advance Manifest System / enhance logistics security` = introduced the Advance Manifest System to enhance logistics security. (물류 안보를 강화하기 위해 적하목록 사전 제출 제도를 도입했다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 'Pre-manifest declaration' 같은 임의 조어 대신 세계관세기구(WCO)와 각국 세관에서 공식 통용되는 `Advance Manifest System`(AMS)을 써야 합니다.
>
> ### 3. `WCO SAFE Framework of Standards` : WCO 세계무역안전 및 원활화 표준
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 세계관세기구(WCO)가 무역의 안전(Security)과 원활화(Facilitation)를 양립시키기 위해 제정한 국제 지침입니다. 각국 세관의 적하목록 사전 제출 제도의 법적·절차적 기반이 됩니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ˈfræmwɜːrk əv ˈstændərdz] 'Framework of'는 연음되어 [프레임워커브]로 읽히며 끝의 'standards' 복수형 어미 [dz]를 놓치지 않아야 합니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `In this context, the WCO adopted “SAFE Framework of Standards to Secure and Facilitate Global Trade”, encouraging worldwide implementation of advance manifest reporting.`
>     - 🇰🇷 이러한 맥락에서 WCO는 "글로벌 무역의 안전 및 원활화를 위한 SAFE 표준 프레임워크"를 채택하여, 전 세계적인 적하목록 사전 보고의 이행을 권장했습니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Are our new clearance procedures aligned with international customs guidelines?
>   > ↳ *우리의 신규 통관 절차가 국제 관세 가이드라인과 일치합니까?*
>   > **B**: Absolutely, they strictly adhere to the WCO SAFE Framework of Standards.
>   > ↳ *물론입니다, WCO SAFE 표준 프레임워크를 엄격히 준수하고 있습니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `abide by [guidelines]` + `WCO guidelines` = abide by WCO guidelines. (WCO 가이드라인을 준수하다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 'Framework' 뒤에 'of Standards'가 수반되는 고유 명칭이므로 임의로 형태를 축약하거나 단어 순서를 바꾸지 않도록 주의합니다.
>
> ### 4. `Commercial Fraud` & `False Labeling of Country of Origin` : 상업적 사기 및 원산지 허위 표시
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 짝퉁(가짜 상품) 유통, 밀수출입, 부정 환급, 라벨 갈이를 통한 원산지 둔갑 등 대외무역 질서를 저해하는 모든 불법 상거래 및 통관 사기 행위를 포괄합니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [kəˈmɜːrʃl frɔːd] / [fɔːls ˈleɪblɪŋ] 'Fraud'의 모음은 [ɔː]이므로 [프로드/프로드]에 가깝고 '플라우드'가 되지 않도록 주의합니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `To curb illegal exports and commercial fraud ranging from fake goods to false labeling of country of origin of goods, by obtaining loading information of export cargo prior to sea or air shipping...`
>     - 🇰🇷 해상 또는 항공 선적 전에 수출 화물의 적재 정보를 확보함으로써 위조 상품에서부터 물품의 원산지 허위 표시에 이르는 불법 수출과 상업적 사기를 근절하기 위하여...
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: How does advance data submission help customs officers?
>   > ↳ *사전 데이터 제출이 세관 공무원들에게 어떤 도움을 줍니까?*
>   > **B**: It allows them to curb commercial fraud and detect false labeling of country of origin before departure.
>   > ↳ *출항 전에 상업적 사기를 억제하고 원산지 허위 표시를 적발할 수 있게 해줍니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `curb [illegal activity] ranging from [A] to [B]` + `commercial fraud / fake goods / false labeling` = curb commercial fraud ranging from fake goods to false labeling. (위조 상품에서 허위 라벨 표시에 이르는 상업 사기를 억제하다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 원산지 허위 표시를 'Fake origin mark'라고 쓰기 쉬우나, 통관 무역 법률상 공식 용어는 `False labeling of country of origin`입니다.
>
> ### 5. `High-risk Consignments` vs `Normal Consignments` : 고위험 화물 vs 일반 화물
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 사전 제출된 적하목록을 토대로 관세청 리스크 관리 시스템이 화물을 위험도에 따라 분류하는 기준입니다. 일반 화물은 신속 통관 및 즉시 반출 혜택을 부여하고, 고위험 화물은 정밀 검사 대상으로 선별합니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [haɪ rɪsk kənˈsaɪnmənts] 'Consignment'의 'g'는 묵음이며, 강세는 두 번째 음절 [saɪn]에 있습니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `...which in turn will improve monitoring high-risk consignments while completing import clearance procedures for normal consignments in advance, leading to their immediate release after unloading instead of being forwarded to bonded warehouses...`
>     - 🇰🇷 ...이는 결국 고위험 화물에 대한 감시를 강화하는 동시에 일반 화물에 대해서는 수입 통관 절차를 사전에 완료하여 보세구역으로 입고되는 대신 하선 후 즉시 반출되도록 이끕니다...
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Is this container required to enter the bonded warehouse?
>   > ↳ *이 컨테이너가 보세창고에 입고되어야 합니까?*
>   > **B**: No, since it is a normal consignment, it qualified for immediate release upon discharge.
>   > ↳ *아닙니다, 일반 화물이기 때문에 하선 후 즉시 반출 승인을 받았습니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `improve monitoring [target]` + `high-risk consignments` = improve monitoring high-risk consignments. (고위험 화물에 대한 감시를 강화하다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 개별 선하증권(B/L) 단위로 탁송되는 무역 화물은 단순 집합명사 'Cargo'보다 법적 위탁 화물 단위인 `Consignment`를 쓰는 것이 더 정밀합니다.
>
> ### 6. `Collector of Customs` / `Customs Collector` : 세관장
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 개별 관세구역(항만·공항) 세관의 행정 수장인 세관장을 가리키는 관세법상의 공식 법률 명칭입니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [kəˈlɛktər əv ˈkʌstəmz] 'Collector of'가 연음되어 [컬렉터러브]로 소리 납니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `If it is required for going through rapid entry and customs clearance procedure, and conducting efficient supervision and control, the collector of customs (hereinafter referred to as “customs collector”) may have the shipping or aviation company... submit the list of passengers, manifest, etc. prior to the entry...`
>     - 🇰🇷 신속한 입항 및 통관 절차를 밟고 효율적인 감시 및 통제를 수행하기 위하여 필요한 경우, 세관장(이하 "세관장"이라 함)은 입항 선사 또는 항공사로 하여금 입항 전에 여객 명부, 적하목록 등을 제출하게 할 수 있다...
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: To whom should the manifest amendment request be addressed?
>   > ↳ *적하목록 정정 신청서는 누구 앞으로 제출해야 합니까?*
>   > **B**: It must be officially submitted to the customs collector for review and approval.
>   > ↳ *검토 및 승인을 받기 위해 공식적으로 관할 세관장에게 제출해야 합니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `obtain a permit from [official]` + `customs collector` = obtain a permit from customs collector. (세관장으로부터 허가를 받다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 세관장을 'Customs Chief'나 'Customs Manager'로 번역하지 마십시오. 법정 공식 명칭은 `Collector of Customs` 또는 `Customs Collector`입니다.
>
> ### 7. `Foreign Trade Vessel or Aircraft` : 외항선 또는 외항기
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 외국과의 무역을 목적으로 개항장(Open port)을 운항하는 선박 및 항공기를 뜻하는 공식 법률 용어입니다.

> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ədˈvæns ˈmænɪfest ˈsɪstəm] 'Advance'의 'v' 발음 시 입술을 가볍게 물어줍니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `In response to the 9/11 attacks in 2001, the Advance Manifest System has been put forward as an answer to enhance logistics security...`
>     - 🇰🇷 2001년 9/11 테러에 대응하여, 물류 안보를 강화하기 위한 해법으로 적하목록 사전 제출 제도가 제안되었습니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Why do we need to comply with the Korea Advance Manifest System?
>   > ↳ *왜 우리가 한국 적하목록 사전 제출 제도를 준수해야 하나요?*
>   > **B**: It helps secure global trade and speeds up customs clearance for normal shipments.
>   > ↳ *글로벌 무역 안보를 확보하고 정상 화물의 관세 통관 절차를 속도감 있게 진행하도록 돕기 때문입니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `introduce [system]` + `the Advance Manifest System` = introduce the Advance Manifest System. (적하목록 사전 제출 제도를 도입하다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: 'Pre-manifest' 대신 국제 표준 공식 명칭인 `Advance Manifest System` 또는 `Advance Manifest Reporting`을 사용해야 합니다.

> ### 10. `Master B/L` vs `House B/L` : 마스터 선하증권 vs 하우스 선하증권
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: Master B/L은 실제 선사/항공사가 발행하는 대표 운송장이며, House B/L은 포워더가 실제 화주(Shipper)에게 직접 발행하는 포워더 운송장입니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ˈmæstər biː-eɪl] / [haʊs biː-eɪl]
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `“Master B/L” is the bill of lading issued by ocean carriers... “House B/L” is the bill of lading or an air waybill that forwarders issue directly to shippers.`
>     - 🇰🇷 "Master B/L"은 해상 운송인이 발행하는 선하증권이며, "House B/L"은 포워더가 화주에게 직접 발행하는 선하증권 또는 항공화물운송장입니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: How do we handle consolidated cargo manifests?
>   > ↳ *혼재 화물 적하목록은 어떻게 처리합니까?*
>   > **B**: Forwarders compile the House B/L list, which is then consolidated into the Master B/L by the carrier.
>   > ↳ *포워더가 House B/L 목록을 작성하고, 운송인이 이를 Master B/L로 통합합니다.*
> - **🔄 패턴 응용 & 교체 연습 (Substitution Drill)**: `[Entity] issues [document] directly to shippers` + `House B/L` = Forwarders issue House B/L directly to shippers. (포워더는 화주에게 직접 하우스 B/L을 발행한다.)
> - **⚠️ 주의사항 & 콩글리시 방지 (Pitfalls & Common Mistakes)**: B/L을 단순히 'Receipt'라고 부르면 안 되며, 두 권한 체계의 차이를 명확히 인지하고 구분해야 합니다.

> ### 11. `Transit Goods` : 환적 화물 / 통과 화물
> - **💡 뉘앙스 & 상황 맥락**: 목적지로 가는 도중 한국을 경유하여 거쳐가는 화물로, 국내 수출입 신고 대상이 아닌 화물을 뜻합니다.
> - **🔊 발음 & 연음 팁 (Pronunciation Tip)**: [ˈtrænzɪt ɡʊdz] 'Transit'의 't' 발음을 명확히 해줍니다.
> - **📝 실전 예문 (Real Examples)**:
>   - 🇺🇸 `“Transit goods” are goods stopping by Korea to be transported to other countries, thus not subject to export/import declaration or return declaration.`
>     - 🇰🇷 "환적 화물"이란 타국으로 운송되기 위해 한국을 거쳐가는 물품으로, 수출입 신고나 반송 신고 대상이 아닌 물품을 말합니다.
> - **💬 대화 스크립트 (Dialogue Context)**:
>   > **A**: Do transit goods require a full import clearance procedure?
>   > ↳ *환적 화물도 전체 수입 통관 절차가 필요한가요?*

1. INTRODUCTION

1-1 Background

Considered as a key document for customs administration, manifest refers to a full list of cargo carried in a sea or air transport-unit which gives particulars such as the description of import/export cargos and supply chains, enabling Customs to conduct risk management, bonded goods management, and loading performance management.

In response to the 9/11 attacks in 2001, the Advance Manifest System has been put forward as an answer to enhance logistics security, and since then it has become a global norm. In this context, the WCO adopted “SAFE Framework of Standards to Secure and Facilitate Global Trade”, encouraging worldwide implementation of advance manifest reporting.

In consideration that major trading partners such as the U.S., EU and China already adopted the Advance Manifest System, the Korea Customs Service (hereinafter referred to as “the KCS”), in cooperation with stakeholders in the supply chain, introduced the Advance Manifest System to abide by WCO guidelines as well as to fulfill its own responsibilities as a governmental agency of duties collection and border protection.

1-2 Reasons for Introducing the Advance Manifest System

(1) To ensure that export goods meet foreign Advance Manifest System requirements by redefining local trade contracts, transportations and customs clearance in compliance with international standards

(2) To curb illegal exports and commercial fraud ranging from fake goods to false labeling of country of origin of goods, by obtaining loading information of export cargo prior to sea or air shipping  

(3) To promote seamless distribution of cargo and reduction of logistics costs by obtaining  loading information of import and transit cargo prior to arrival, which in turn will improve monitoring high-risk consignments while completing import clearance procedures for normal consignments in advance, leading to their immediate release after unloading instead of being forwarded to bonded warehouses  

1-3 Legal Basis

Legal basis under the provisions of the Customs Act related to the Advance Manifest System (hereinafter referred to as “the AMS”) is stated below:

(Article 135, Clause 2) If it is required for going through rapid entry and customs clearance procedure, and conducting efficient supervision and control, the collector of customs (hereinafter referred to as “customs collector”) may have the shipping or aviation company to which the ship or aircraft entering into the port submit the list of passengers, manifest, etc. prior to the entry, under the conditions as prescribed by the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service. 

(Article 136, Clause 2) When a foreign trade vessel or aircraft desires to depart from an open port, the captain thereof shall, prior to departure, obtain a departure permit from customs collector. The captain of a vessel or aircraft shall submit a list of goods boarded at the open port. 

(Article 140, Clause 2) When goods are to be loaded to or unloaded from any foreign trade vessels or aircraft, a declaration thereon shall be made to customs collector and confirmation thereon shall be obtained on the spot from customs officers.

Essential particulars related to the AMS, including timeline of submission, specification templates, preparation method and application for corrections should comply with the “Notification of Loading and Unloading Procedure of Entry and Departure for Bonded Cargo (2011-7, March 18, 2011)” by the KCS.

2. BASICS

2-1 General Principles

Ocean and air carriers should comply with timeline of manifest submission, and should complete and submit manifest which should exactly correspond to the list of cargo on board.

Manifest should be submitted in compliance with required items, specification and instructions stipulated in the “Message Implementation Guide” issued by the KCS, and also, ocean and air carriers, in cooperation with forwarders, should be careful not to have any discrepancies in the contents of submitted manifest. 

In case cargo on board is found to be not the same as cargo requested for transportation or is identified as prohibited item for export or import according to the Customs Law, carriers should report promptly the irregularity to customs collector 

2-2 Scope

This guideline applies to export, import, transit, returning and temporarily unloaded cargo. 

In spite of the regulation mentioned above, this guideline does not apply to freight remaining onboard and leaving for foreign country as its final destination without being unloaded from  vessel or aircraft in the territory of Korea. However, exception may be made by Customs’ own determination in case it considers surveillance or inspection is necessary for aforementioned shipments. 

2-3 Definition of Terms

The definition of terms used in this guideline provides that:

"Manifest” is a list of goods comprising cargo carried in a sea or air transport-unit with the Master B/L information remarked by ocean or air carriers, written and compiled in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide” issued by the KCS. It is called “Consolidated Cargo Manifest” if forwarders remark the House B/L list.

“Person obliged to submit manifests” includes maritime shipping lines operating international trade ships (including freight forwarders, hereinafter referred to as “ocean carriers”) and airlines (in case of freight forwarder and joint operating airline, actual operating airline; and hereinafter referred to as “air carriers”).

“Person in charge of preparation of manifests” comes under one of the following:

Ocean or air carriers collecting and transporting export and import cargo;

In the case of joint operation, ocean or air carriers chartering vessels and aircraft (including freight forwarders); or

In the case of consolidated cargos, forwarders (including its agents)

“Master B/L” is the bill of lading issued by ocean carriers or an air waybill issued by air carriers.

“House B/L” is the bill of lading or an air waybill that forwarders issue directly to shippers.

“Transit goods” are goods stopping by Korea to be transported to other countries, thus not subject to export/import declaration or return declaration.

“Electronic document” is electronic data designed for transmission between computers, written and compiled in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide” issued by the KSC

2-4 Korea Manifest System

The KCS runs the Korea Manifest System (hereinafter referred to as “the KMS”) for better processing of acceptance, handling and risk management of manifests submitted by ocean carriers or air carriers.

After compiling and completing  manifest, ocean and air carriers should submit it to the KMS through message broker who is designated by the KCS to perform data validation between the KMS and submitter. 

3. DETAILED GUIDELINES

3-1 Person Obliged to Submit Manifests

Ocean and air carriers are obliged to submit manifests for export and import cargo, and are legally responsible for any breach of submission deadlines as well as the content, accuracy and completeness of submitted data. Local freight forwarders take responsibility of compiling and correcting export House B/L data as an entity in charge of compiling consolidated cargo manifests.

With regard to consolidated cargo, carriers should collect and compile House B/L data from freight forwarders (, including those who handle consolidated cargo in the country of exportation) and submit it after final verification to the KCS in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide”.

Carriers can entrust the task of manifest compiling and submission to message broker as authorized by the KSC according to the Article 327-3 of the Customs Law.

3-3-2 Instructions on Goods Description 

Carriers and freight forwarders should pay special attention to goods description during manifest preparation in order that common mistakes will not be made. 

The column for “Good Description” should not be filled in with any meaningless numbers, marks or general terms such as:

Meaningless numbers and marks: 0, 1, 11, A, AB, %, $, #, @, etc.

General terms: PARTS, SAMPLE, ACCESSORY, etc.

Goods description should be described in specific and precise manner as used in trade deals in order that the KCS can identify the nature of goods. “Examples of Goods Descriptions Accepted or not Accepted” in Appendix Table 1 can be referred for this purpose. 

3-4 Data Verification

Customs collector will verify the content, accuracy and completeness of declared manifest, and submitted data can be accepted by the KMS only when declared contents are validated to be free of error.

When an error is found in declared manifest in the process of verification, customs collectors should inform concerned carriers of such errors by electronic means. In such case, the carriers should submit amended version of  manifest. 

In order to efficiently perform data verification on declared manifests, the KCS may designate a data verification operator among message brokers and, in this case, should provide the operator with such information as enforcement regulations, items to verify, verifying methods and procedures.

Data verification operator must provide enough information on enforcement regulations and the operation of verification system to users such as ocean and air carriers and forwarders. 

3-5 Do-Not-Load Actions

Do-Not-Load (hereinafter referred to as “DNL”) notification may be issued by Customs against such cargo that is considered risky for security purpose by the KCS. 

DNL notification applies only to deep-sea containerized shipments for which manifest should be submitted at least 24 hours prior to loading. 

 

3-6 Cancellation of Declaration

In principle, ocean and air carriers cannot withdraw their manifests submitted to the KCS.

Customs collector may permit cancellation of declaration after reviewing supporting documents submitted by carriers, when the reason for cancellation falls into one of the following categories:

Non-arrival of vessels or aircraft due to bad weather or accidents, etc.;

Change in operating schedule of vessels or aircrafts;

Transmission error of Manifest Reference Numbers (MRN); or

If customs collector determines that such a provision is necessary.

If ocean and air carriers wish to withdraw their manifests, they should submit an application for cancellation to customs collector, in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide (Appendix Form 5)” issued by the KCS.

3-7 Application for Amendments

In case ocean and air carriers and freight forwarders intend to amend parts of their manifests after submission, they should submit an application for amendment to customs collector, in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide (Appendix Form 6 and 7)” issued by the KCS.

Customs collectors may permit the amendment of submitted manifest, and check relevant cargo on the spot if deemed necessary, after reviewing supporting documents submitted by carriers, when the reason for amendment or the part to be corrected falls into one of the following categories:

Addition of B/L to correct omission on manifests;

Consignee(Shipper);

Forwarder;

Freight classification and attributes of cargo; or

If customs collector determines that such a provision is necessary for risk management.

In case that cargo is classified high-risk one by the KMS, submitted amendment request should be accepted only after inspection is made on it and the result is filed by Customs.

3-8 Declaration of Unloading

In case ocean and air carriers wish to unload cargo, a declaration of unloading should be submitted to customs collector, in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide (Appendix Form 8 and 9)” issued by the KCS.

Ocean and air carriers, or message broker designated by the KCS, should provide information on import cargo manifest by ship or flight number, so that freight forwarders can apply for unloading or bonded-warehousing plan, etc. for import cargo.  

If unloaded cargo does not match with declared manifest, ocean and air carriers should submit the report of unloading result to customs collector, in accordance with the “Message Implementation Guide (Appendix Form 10 and 11)” issued by the KCS.

3-9 On-site Inspection of Export Goods

In line with the implementation of the AMS, and as part of strengthened risk management efforts, customs collector shall do special inspection on selected export goods at the place of sea-/ airports of loading to verify actual loading status and declared information of those cargo. 

Ocean and air carriers and freight forwarders should submit export goods permission number (EPN), which is one of particulars of export cargo manifest, to the KMS before loading (except for cargo of which manifests should be submitted 24 hours before loading).

If a cargo is selected by the KCS as an object of special export inspection, ocean and air carriers should request for inspection to Customs when it is checked in container terminals at sea ports or cargo terminals at airports. 

When ocean and air carriers load cargo and leave sea-/airport without requesting for inspection to Customs even when the loaded cargo are selected for inspection, they shall be penalized for violating Article 265 of the Customs Act, Inspection of Goods or Transport, etc.

Detailed operating procedures for inspecting export goods at the port of loading should be followed in accordance with the “Detailed Regulations about Inspecting Goods at the Port of Loading” issued by the KCS.

4. ENFORCEMENT MEASURES

4-1 Date of Enforcement

The AMS will be enforced from December 1, 2011 for maritime export cargo and air export/import cargo.

Regarding maritime import cargo and transit cargo (only for departure), the AMS will be enforced from a specific date designated by the Commissioner of the KCS.

4-2 ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

When ocean or air carriers fail to submit manifest declarations before the due date of submission, they shall be penalized for violating Article 276 of the Customs Act, False Declaration.

For smooth transition to the AMS and improvement in trade practices among ocean and air carriers, freight forwarders, exporters and importers, administrative sanctions (fines and penalties) will be suspended until June 30. 2012.
